How the edge is calculated

bet365's odds are treated as the true market. We strip bet365's bookmaker margin to get the real win probability p = (1 / bet365_odds) ÷ market_overround, then compute the expected value of backing the same outcome on sportsbet: edge = p × sportsbet_odds − 1. A row appears only when that edge is at least the configured threshold. Bigger sportsbet odds than bet365's fair price = sportsbet has mispriced it in your favour.

Odds move fast and books limit/void winning value bettors. This is an analysis tool, not financial advice. Bet responsibly.
